# संत ज्ञानेश्वर महाराज / Sant Dnyaneshwar Maharaj

A reading and research portal on Sant Dnyaneshwar Maharaj, offered on the occasion of the Sanjeevan Samadhi Sohala, 2026.
It is part of the "Maharashtra Sants" library. The front of the site is a set of experiences (the Pasaydan, the samadhi as Namdev tells it, a reading room, his life as
a river journey, the wari, the circle of saints); the research archive sits behind the workshop door. The old `preview/`
address forwards to the root. The site is plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript and JSON data, served by GitHub Pages
from the `main` branch: https://gandhar99.github.io/Dnyaneshwar-Maharaj/

## What is shown, and how sure it is
Every statement on the site carries its evidence status and its sources. Rows marked provisional were prepared in
November 2026 and await replacement by the library's own research. Themes on poems, the uses of names and the collated
gatha text come from unchecked machine reading and are labelled as such. Nothing has been checked against page images.
The page "how this was made" on the site gives the full account.
